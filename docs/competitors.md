the most commonly reported ones. Check each vendor's pricing page before quoting them publicly.

## Where we beat every free tier

1. **Design + schedule in one free tool.** No competitor's free plan includes a real pin designer with
   premium templates and a brand kit.
2. **No monthly post cap.** Tailwind Free caps around 5 posts a month, Buffer Free at 10 queued per channel, and the
   native scheduler at roughly 10 queued.
3. **Free AI everywhere.** Images, titles, descriptions, alt text and board ideas all work with no key. Users who
   want better models can bring their own OpenAI-compatible key.
4. **Bulk mode and auto-schedule for free.** Competitors keep these behind paid plans.
5. **Anti-spam spreading.** The same link is automatically spread across different boards and days.

## Where competitors are still ahead

- Multiple Pinterest accounts and team seats (Tailwind, Buffer, Later).
- Instagram/Facebook cross-posting (Buffer, Later, Tailwind).
- Tailwind Communities.
- Deep analytics dashboards.

## Naming note

Pinterest's developer brand guidelines ask apps not to use "Pin" or "Pinterest" in their product name or domain. Our
name, **Tackly**, avoids both. Quick searches found no existing Pinterest tool called Tackly.
